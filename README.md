## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a full-time scholarship student pursuing a degree in **Systems and Networks Computer Engineering** at Universidad Gerardo Barrios (UGB), maintaining an outstanding academic performance (GPA equivalent > 9.4). My focus is on **software architecture**, **multi-engine database administration**, **Linux infrastructure (DevOps)**, and the **integration of hardware and Artificial Intelligence**.

Concurrently, I work as an **Academic Tutor and Instructor**, guiding first-year students in Programming Fundamentals (Python), Object-Oriented Programming (OOP), Systems Logic, Data Structures, and Discrete Mathematics.

I hold a **Huawei Python & Data Science** certification (90/100), have completed continuous training in **Cloud Infrastructure and AI** (EEST School), and possess accreditations from **AWS Academy** (Cloud Foundations) and **Cisco Networking Academy** (IT Essentials). My future goal is to pursue a Master's and Ph.D. oriented towards Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence for university teaching and research.
